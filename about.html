Lord Buddha Education Foundation

Established in 1998, Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) stands as the pioneer First IT College of Nepal. Located in the heart of Kathmandu at Maitidevi, LBEF has educated thousands of tech innovators, software engineers, and business leaders across South Asia.

In proud academic affiliation with Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU), Malaysia, LBEF offers internationally accredited undergraduate and graduate degrees. Hoop Fest represents our commitment to holistic student development, competitive athletic spirit, and mental resilience.
