Official Rulebook

FIBA Guidelines & Campus Code

Key competition statutes enforced across all LBEF Hoop Fest games.

timer Match Duration & Shot Clock

All 5v5 matches consist of four (4) quarters of 10 minutes stop-clock play. Halftime interval is 10 minutes, with 2-minute breaks between quarters. 24-second shot clock applies, resetting to 14 seconds upon an offensive rebound.

restart_alt Overtime Protocol

If scores are tied at the expiration of 4th quarter regulation time, an extra period of five (5) minutes will be played. Consecutive 5-minute overtimes continue until a clear winner emerges.

sports Fouls, Free Throws & Disqualification

A player who commits five (5) personal or technical fouls is disqualified from the remainder of the game. Team foul bonus triggers two free throws on the fifth team foul in each quarter.

gavel Disciplinary & Zero Tolerance Policy

Unsportsmanlike conduct, verbal abuse of referees or volunteers, or violent physical altercations will result in immediate player expulsion and potential team disqualification certified by the Athletic Advisory Board.
