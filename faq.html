Frequently Asked Questions

Find detailed guidance on student eligibility, team registration deadlines, admission rules, and arena facilities.

search
Who is eligible to participate in LBEF Hoop Fest 2026? expand_more
Any currently enrolled undergraduate or graduate student at Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) or invited partner institutions such as Asia Pacific University (APU), Malaysia is eligible. All players must present their valid physical college smart card prior to entering the court.
What is the registration deadline and fee structure? expand_more
Team roster registrations close on October 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM NPT. The illustrative tournament fee is NPR 5,000 per squad for 5v5 varsity competition, which includes official tournament uniforms, medical coverage, and officiating charges.
Which official rules govern tournament play? expand_more
All games strictly follow FIBA official basketball rules. Matches consist of four 10-minute stop-clock periods. In the event of a tie at the end of regulation, 5-minute overtime periods are played until a victor is determined. Five personal fouls result in player disqualification.
Where is the venue and do spectators require tickets? expand_more
All matches are hosted at the LBEF Sports Complex Indoor Arena in Maitidevi, Kathmandu, Nepal. Admission is completely free for all bona fide college students, faculty, and accredited guests upon presentation of university identification.
Can a student athlete play for more than one team? expand_more
No. A player can only be registered on a single varsity roster. Registering across multiple teams will disqualify the athlete from the tournament immediately under LBEF sports council regulations.
How can students get involved as event volunteers? expand_more
Interested students can navigate to the Registration Page and select the "Volunteer Application" tab. Volunteers assist with table officiating, scoreboard tracking, first aid dispatch, and media recording.
What facilities and medical amenities are provided at the arena? expand_more
Maitidevi Arena features high-grip shock-absorbing hardwood, two full-sized regulation courts, player locker rooms with showers, dedicated first-aid stations with certified sports physiotherapists, and two-wheeler campus parking.
